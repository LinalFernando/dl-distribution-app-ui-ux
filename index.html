DL Distribution App – UI/UX

A mobile Distribution and Order Management application designed in Figma with a focus on simple workflows, intuitive navigation, and a clean user experience.

Project Overview

DL Distribution is a mobile application UI/UX concept designed to support distribution and sales-related business activities. The design focuses on making customer management, product selection, orders, sales, and returns simple and efficient.

  • Customer registration and management
  • Product catalogue and product selection
  • Order placement and checkout
  • Sales and order history
  • Returnable product management
  • Return processing and confirmation
  • Customer outstanding balance information
  • Search, filtering, and date selection
